Good to know

Questions, answered.

Is the hospital open at night and on holidays?

Yes. We’re open 24 hours a day, all seven days a week.

What should I do if someone has chest pain?

Don’t wait. Call us on 78426 52341 or the ambulance on 108 immediately, keep the person calm and seated, and bring them to the hospital.

Which services do you offer?

Cardiology with ECG and 2D Echo, emergency care and ICU, general medicine, diabetes and thyroid care, gastroenterology, general surgery with an in-house Operation Theatre, anaesthesiology, maternity care, laboratory, pharmacy, nursing, outpatient services, general check-ups and hospice care.

Is the treatment affordable?

Yes — patients regularly mention high-quality treatment at low cost. Please call us for specific charges.

Is the hospital wheelchair accessible?

Yes. There’s a wheelchair-accessible entrance, seating and car park.

Where exactly are you?

On Balaji Road in Tuni, opposite Bank of Baroda — 6-7-21/3, Tuni, Andhra Pradesh 533401.
